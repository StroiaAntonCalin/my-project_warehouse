**Status**: Minimal prototype persona — draft  
**Created**: 2026-09-07  
**Evidence basis**: Approved Signal, context, hypothesis, and refined concept

## Persona: Individual Gym Member

### Snapshot

An individual gym member who wants to organize planned exercises for the week and keep the plan current. The member uses a browser and is not assumed to have a trainer, administrator, or shared schedule role. **[OBS]**

### Need statement

> I need a quick, clear way to see my week and update exercises for each day so I can keep my workout plan organized.

This need statement is a prototype assumption derived from the confirmed product request and is not yet validated through user research. **[INF]**

### Goals

- Log in and reach a personal schedule.
- Understand the seven-day plan at a glance.
- Add an exercise to the correct day.
- Edit or remove an exercise when plans change.
- Know whether changes were successfully saved.

### Frustrations to test

- A schedule view may feel crowded on a small screen.
- Too many required exercise fields may slow down planning.
- Unclear save or error states may cause uncertainty or lost work.
- A user may expect to see only their own schedule and may distrust the product if that boundary is unclear.

These are validation prompts, not confirmed research findings. **[INF]**

### Behaviors and context

- Uses the product after logging in.
- Plans or updates exercises for one or more weekdays.
- May use desktop or mobile browser sizes.
- Needs keyboard-accessible controls and clear status feedback.

### Success signals

- Identifies the correct weekday card without help.
- Completes add, edit, remove, and save tasks.
- Can recover after a failed save without losing entered data.
- Reports that the scheduler helps organize workouts.

### Out of scope for this persona

This persona does not represent trainers, gym administrators, shared-schedule collaborators, payment users, or users requiring medical guidance. **[OBS]**
